Our Story

Opalescent Insurance Agency was founded with one simple goal—making insurance accessible, affordable and easy to understand. From a small office serving local families, we have grown into a trusted insurance agency offering a wide range of insurance products across Kenya. We believe insurance should give confidence, security and peace of mind rather than confusion.
